# Implementation Plan: Reconcile HA Monthly Stats With Cumulative Totals

**Branch**: `011-ha-monthly-totals` | **Date**: 2026-05-30 | **Spec**: [spec.md](spec.md)
**Input**: Feature specification from `/specs/011-ha-monthly-totals/spec.md`

## Summary

Switch the monthly cumulative-total derivation from arithmetic sum-of-daily-deltas to `HA monthly sum[month] − HA monthly sum[prev_month]` (Option A).
The HA monthly statistics payload is already fetched on every load but currently discarded; this feature consumes it.
Monthly fetch range extended by one month at the leading edge so January totals can subtract December-of-prior-year.
Negative monthly deltas clamped for `total_increasing` (counter-reset anomaly), passed through as-is for `total` (legitimate net export).
Cumulative-row min/avg/max stay as today (derived from daily values per per-row `show_zero`, established in feature 010).
Expression-row totals stay as arithmetic daily sums (no HA monthly stat exists for formulas).
Measurement-row min/avg/max recompute behaviour unchanged (commit `58ca61d`).
Constitution Principle III amended (MINOR bump 2.0.0 → 2.1.0) to codify the full set of monthly-derivation rules in one place.
